Controls Guide

How To Play

1. Hold power

Press and hold the power button to increase shot strength. Release to shoot.

2. Adjust angle

Hold the angle button until the shot arc looks right for the distance.

3. Track the turn

Use the HUD to check current team, player and shot count before each attempt.

4. Score smart

Long arc shots can become three-pointers when the ball exits above the hoop before scoring.
